Summary

The developer will build an AI scheduling engine that automatically generates optimal, conflict-free class schedules for the department. The Dean gives instructions through a GPT-style chat, and the system learns from past schedules and the Dean's adjustments through machine learning.

This quotation updates the earlier ₱15,000 quote. It adds the features requested by the panel during the proposal defense: machine learning, Dean-only access and stronger data security.

What the system will do

Schedule generation

  • Automatically generates a complete class schedule from subjects, sections, faculty, rooms, time slots and the department's rules
  • Guarantees no faculty, room or section conflicts, and respects room capacity, room type (lecture/laboratory), faculty availability and maximum teaching load
  • Optimizes for faculty preferred days and times, minimal vacant gaps and balanced teaching loads
  • Re-generates a new optimal schedule when something changes, such as a faculty member becoming unavailable

For the Dean

  • Give instructions in plain language through a GPT-style chat (for example: "Generate the 1st semester schedule, and don't schedule Prof. X on Fridays")
  • Review the schedule in a spreadsheet-like workspace and a timetable view
  • See a dashboard of faculty workload, room utilization, subject distribution and schedule quality
  • Export schedules per section, faculty or room, and faculty load reports, to PDF and Excel

Machine learning

  • Recommends the best-fit faculty for each subject, based on specialization and past assignments
  • Learns faculty and department patterns, such as preferred time slots, rooms and loads
  • Improves every semester by learning from finalized schedules and the Dean's adjustments

Security and privacy

  • Only the Dean can access the system, with a password and a one-time code (multi-factor authentication)
  • Faculty names and personal details are replaced with codes before anything is sent to the AI service
  • Every login and change is recorded in an audit log
  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and handled under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)

Technology and AI approach

The system uses a hybrid AI design: the scheduling engine guarantees a conflict-free schedule, machine learning makes it smarter each semester, and generative AI lets the Dean work in plain language.

PartTechnologyRole
FrontendReact + TypeScript (Next.js), Tailwind CSS, AG Grid, FullCalendar, RechartsChat, scheduling workspace, timetable, dashboard
BackendNode.js + TypeScriptAccounts, data, reports, connects the AI and the engine
DatabasePostgreSQL with PrismaFaculty, subjects, sections, rooms, schedules
Scheduling enginePython + Google OR-Tools (CP-SAT solver)Generates the optimal, conflict-free schedule
Machine learningPython (scikit-learn) and NLP text matchingFaculty–subject recommendations, preference learning
Generative AIOpenAI GPT or Anthropic Claude via API (Standard); open-source model such as Llama, Qwen or Gemma on the school's server (Premium)Understands instructions, explains results
  • The AI does not invent schedules. It turns the Dean's request into inputs for the scheduling engine, then explains the result. Every schedule comes from the department's real data and rules.
  • Paid API accounts only. Data sent through the providers' paid APIs is not used to train their models by default.
  • Works with one semester of data. The engine needs no history. Machine learning starts from the available semester plus faculty profiles and a preference survey, and improves every semester after.

What the developer needs from the proponents

Within week 1, in the data-gathering template

  • At least 1 semester of past class schedules (more semesters improve the machine learning)
  • Faculty list with specializations, educational background, teaching loads and availability
  • Room list with capacity and type (lecture/laboratory)
  • Curriculum and subjects per program and year level
  • The department's approved scheduling rules

During development

  • Faculty preference survey results (preferred days, time slots and subjects), Standard and Premium only
  • One contact person to answer questions and approve each phase
  • Feedback during the testing phase on any wrong or unclear results
  • Access to the school's server, Premium only

Not included

  • AI usage, hosting and domain fees (see Monthly running cost)
